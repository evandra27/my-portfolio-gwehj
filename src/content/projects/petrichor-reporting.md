## Project Overview

In urban areas, finding reliable and trustworthy handymen for household maintenance (electrical, AC, plumbing) is often time-consuming and prone to price speculation. **SiBeres** was conceptualized as an all-in-one digital marketplace that connects homeowners with verified professional handymen. 

By offering transparent upfront pricing, online scheduling, emergency dispatch, and an integrated tool rental service, SiBeres addresses core urban household pain points while opening broader economic opportunities for informal technicians.

## Responsibilities & Process

Working in a cross-functional team, my core focus centered on **UX Research, Business Value Proposition Mapping, and Mobile Interface Design**:

* **User & Market Research:** Formulated survey questionnaires and conducted direct interviews with urban households to extract key pain points regarding home repair services.
* **Value Proposition & Empathy Mapping:** Constructed Customer Profiles, Value Proposition Maps, and Empathy Maps to map user pains against platform solutions.
* **Service Feature Framework:** Modeled core feature flows including verified handyman booking, upfront cost estimation, 7-day service warranty, and hardware tool rental.
* **UI Prototyping & Flow Mapping:** Designed interactive high-fidelity mobile screen flows in Figma covering user registration, booking scheduling, real-time tracking, and tool rental payments.

## Design Process & Key Solutions

### 1. Research Insights & Value Proposition
From user survey findings, **over 55% of respondents** expressed high interest in an on-demand handyman platform, citing unstandardized pricing and lack of trust as primary hurdles.

![SiBeres Value Proposition Canvas](/images/SiBeresValueMap.jpg)
*Figure 1.0: Value Proposition Canvas mapping user pains (price uncertainty, untrusted quality) to platform features.*

**Core Value Pillars:**
* **Verified Handymen:** Eliminates fraud risks through identity and skill verification.
* **Upfront Price Transparency:** Provides cost estimates prior to job confirmation to prevent price haggling.
* **7-Day Work Guarantee:** Delivers peace of mind with guaranteed follow-up repairs if issues persist.

---

### 2. Core App Features & Workflow

![SiBeres App Interface & Features](/images/SiBeresUI.png)
*Figure 2.0: Core UI flows for Handyman Selection, Transparent Scheduling, and Tool Rental Marketplace.*

* **On-Demand Handyman Service (Jasa Tukang):** Users can filter technicians by category (AC, Electrical, Plumbing), view rating/reviews, and choose explicit arrival schedules.
* **Hardware Tool Rental (Sewa Alat):** An integrated marketplace allowing users or technicians to rent specialized tools (e.g., electric drills, ladders, vacuum cleaners) with daily pricing and security deposit safeguards.
* **Order Tracking & Confirmation:** Real-time order progress monitoring from dispatch to completion with full fee breakdown (service fee, transport fee, total).
