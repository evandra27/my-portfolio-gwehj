## Project Overview

For this first-semester university case study, my partner and I collaborated to design a mobile app tailored for K-pop fans to streamline content access and enhance community engagement. Operating as a two-person team, I served as the primary UX Designer, leading the research and structural framework, while my teammate focused on visual execution as the UI Designer.

## Responsibilities & Process

My core responsibility was ensuring the app directly solved key fan pain points through a user-centered design approach. I conducted target audience research to map out user personas, journey maps, and intuitive task flows. From there, I established the app's information architecture and translated complex fan interactions into clean, low-to-mid fidelity wireframes to validate navigation paths before handing them off for visual design.

- **Conducted user research and target audience analysis** to identify K-pop fans' key pain points and motivations.
- **Created user personas, user journey maps, and task flows** to define seamless navigation paths.
- **Developed information architecture and wireframes** (low-to-mid fidelity) for core app features.
- **Gathered feedback through initial usability testing** to validate design decisions and optimize overall user experience.

## Design Process & Architecture

To transform fan research insights into an intuitive mobile experience, I structured the application hierarchy and interaction flows to ensure seamless navigation across all core features.

---

### 1. Information Architecture & Navigation Map
I mapped out a clear 4-pillar navigation structure rooted from the main Home dashboard. This structure minimizes navigation friction, allowing K-pop fans to easily transition between community interaction, AI support, e-commerce, and account management.

![StarWave Navigation Map](/images/StarwaveNavigationMap.png)
*Figure 1.0: Information Architecture mapping out the 4 primary feature pillars (Interact, Chatbot, Shop, Profile).*

**Key Navigation Highlights:**
* **Authentication Flow:** Straightforward Splash → Onboarding → Sign Up/In path to get users onboarded quickly.
* **Interact Pillar:** Dedicated space for fan engagement, consisting of community **Feed** (with direct **Upload Feed** capabilities) and **Artist** updates.
* **Chatbot Pillar:** Direct access to an interactive **Chat room** for instant fan support and inquiry.
* **Shop Pillar:** Streamlined merchandise discovery leading directly from **Detail Merchandise** to a friction-free **Checkout**.
* **Profile Pillar:** Centralized hub for user settings (**Edit Profile**), assistance (**FAQ**), and app information (**About Us**).

---

### 2. User Flows & Low-Fidelity Wireframes
Before translating concepts into final visual design, I built end-to-end low-to-mid fidelity wireframes. This allowed us to map out comprehensive wireflows, validate touch targets, and refine edge cases across all primary user journeys.

![StarWave Low-Fidelity Wireframes](/images/StarwaveWireframe.png)
*Figure 2.0: Low-to-mid fidelity wireframe suite mapping out complete interaction flows and modal states.*

**UX Decisions & Refinements:**
* **Consistent Bottom Navigation:** Kept primary actions accessible within thumb range across all main screens.
* **Streamlined Checkout Wireflow:** Reduced steps required to select merchandise and complete payment to maximize conversion.
* **Interactive Feed Layout:** Optimized card layout for fan-generated content to encourage upload frequency and community engagement.
